Privacy

Privacy Policy

This page explains how information may be collected and used when you visit the Orton Heritage website or contact us through it.

Last updated: 14 September 2026

Information you provide

If you contact Orton Heritage through the website enquiry form or by email, we may receive information such as your name, email address, project or site location, and the information you include in your message.

We use this information to respond to your enquiry, communicate with you about potential or current work, and maintain appropriate business records.

Website analytics

This website uses Google Analytics 4 to help us understand how the website is used. Google Analytics may collect information such as pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser information, referral source, and interactions with the website. It may use cookies or similar technologies for this purpose.

We use this information in aggregate to understand website performance, improve content and measure enquiries. We do not use Google Analytics to intentionally collect information that directly identifies you.

Contact form

The website enquiry form is processed using Formspree. Information submitted through the form is transmitted through Formspree so that it can be delivered to Orton Heritage. Formspree may process that information in accordance with its own privacy and data-handling practices.

Website hosting and third-party services

The website is hosted using GitHub Pages. As with most website hosting services, technical information such as IP addresses, request details and device information may be processed as part of providing and securing the service.

Third-party services used by this website include Google Analytics, Formspree and GitHub Pages. Their handling of information is also subject to their own privacy policies and terms.

How we handle information

We take reasonable steps to handle personal information responsibly and use it only for purposes connected with your enquiry, our professional services, website operation, analytics and legitimate business administration.

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes, including where records are needed for professional, administrative or legal reasons.

Your choices

You can choose not to submit information through the website form and contact us directly by email instead. You can also control or block cookies through your browser settings, although this may affect some website functionality.

Contact

If you have a question about privacy or information you have provided to us, contact:

patrick@ortonheritage.com

We may update this policy from time to time as the website, services or legal requirements change.
