Heritage services · Brisbane & Queensland

Clear advice for complex places.

We provide heritage and design advice from early project planning through to approvals, documentation and works. Based in Brisbane, we work across Queensland and Northern New South Wales with a practical focus: understand the place, identify what matters and help the project respond with confidence.

01

Heritage Impact Statements & Assessments

Evidence-based Heritage Impact Statements and assessments for proposed work to heritage places, sites within heritage overlays and development affecting heritage settings. We explain significance, assess likely impacts and clearly document the design response for planning and approval processes in Brisbane and across Queensland. Learn more about Heritage Impact Statements →

02

Character Impact Statements & Advice

Assessment and design advice for development within traditional building character areas and character precincts, including Brisbane projects. We help projects respond to established streetscape, scale, form and pattern while accommodating contemporary requirements.

03

Conservation Management Plans

Conservation Management Plans that bring together a place's history, fabric, significance and conservation needs into a clear framework for future decisions, maintenance, adaptation and change. Learn more about Conservation Management Plans →

04

Heritage & Design Advice

Early and ongoing advice to owners, architects and consultant teams so heritage considerations inform the design rather than being addressed at the end. We review options, identify constraints and opportunities, and help shape heritage-sensitive responses.

05

3D Scanning, Photogrammetry & Digital Recording

Digital capture of buildings, fabric and site conditions using contemporary recording techniques. These tools can support analysis, measured documentation, condition records and a more complete understanding of complex existing places.

06

Heritage Research & Significance Assessment

Historical research and assessment to establish a clear understanding of a place, its development and its heritage significance. We use documentary research, site observation and analysis of fabric and context to inform heritage advice, conservation planning and development decisions.

Common questions

Heritage advice before problems arise.

When might I need a Heritage Impact Statement?

A Heritage Impact Statement is commonly requested where proposed work affects a heritage-listed place, a place within a heritage overlay, or the setting of a heritage place. The exact requirement depends on the relevant planning scheme, approval pathway and scope of work. Read more about our Heritage Impact Statement service.

Can you help before a development application is lodged?

Yes. Early heritage advice can help identify significant fabric, likely approval issues and opportunities before the design is fixed, which can make later assessment and documentation more straightforward.

Do you only work in Brisbane?

We are based in Brisbane and work across Queensland and Northern New South Wales, including local and State heritage places, character areas, institutional sites and residential projects.
