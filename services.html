Our services

Clear advice for complex places.

We provide heritage, conservation and design advice from early project planning through to approvals, documentation and works. Our focus is practical: understand the place, identify what matters and help the project respond with confidence.

01

Heritage Impact Statements & Assessments

Evidence-based assessment of proposed work to heritage places, sites within heritage overlays and development affecting heritage settings. We explain significance, assess likely impacts and clearly document the design response for planning and approval processes.

02

Character Impact Statements & Advice

Assessment and design advice for development within traditional building character and character precincts. We help projects respond to established streetscape, scale, form and pattern while accommodating contemporary requirements.

03

Conservation Management Plans

Conservation Management Plans that bring together a place's history, fabric, significance and conservation needs into a clear framework for future decisions, maintenance, adaptation and change.

04

Heritage & Design Advice

Early and ongoing advice to owners, architects and consultant teams so heritage considerations inform the design rather than being addressed at the end. We review options, identify constraints and opportunities, and help shape heritage-sensitive responses.

05

Conservation, Restoration & Adaptive Reuse

Advice on conserving significant fabric, repairing traditional materials and adapting existing buildings for new uses. Our approach prioritises retention, compatible intervention and practical long-term use.

06

Heritage Listing, Boundary Advice & Submissions

Review of proposed heritage listings, significance assessments and heritage boundaries, including clear written submissions where a listing or mapped extent requires closer examination.

07

Archival & Photographic Recording

Systematic recording of heritage places before, during or after change. This can include measured information, high-quality photography, annotated documentation and archival records suited to project or approval requirements.

08

3D Scanning, Photogrammetry & Digital Recording

Digital capture of buildings, fabric and site conditions using contemporary recording techniques. These tools can support analysis, measured documentation, condition records and a more complete understanding of complex existing places.

09

Condition, Remediation & Heritage Research

Targeted historical research, significance assessment and condition-based advice to support repair strategies, remediation planning, maintenance and informed decision-making.
